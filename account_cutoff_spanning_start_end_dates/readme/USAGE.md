On an accrual cut-off, the *Cut-off Spanning Account* is proposed from
the company settings and can be changed. After generating the lines, the
column *Spanning* shows the lines whose period spans the cut-off date.

When the journal entry is created, the counterpart of the spanning lines
is recorded on the *Cut-off Spanning Account*, and the counterpart of
the other lines on the *Cut-off Account*. If the *Cut-off Spanning
Account* is empty, all the lines use the *Cut-off Account*.
